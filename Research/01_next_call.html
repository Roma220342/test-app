05 Risks vs how we win

Risks

  • No hands-on access (VPN-only) — we design partly blind. Mitigate with the walkthrough + analyst recording + sharp questions.
  • Domain ambiguity — if the analyst can't clarify award rule/validity, our labels stay guesses.
  • Sensitivity about their own work — they built the current screens/AI mockups; "rebuild" framing can sting.
  • Rebrand dependency — colours/type may shift under the separate branding partner; don't over-commit visuals.
  • Scope creep — nav, advanced mode, dashboard all tempt; the trial is the wizard.

How we win

  • Respect their structure — "your flow is sound; we make it feel as capable as it is." Lowers risk, flatters the work.
  • Two demo-ready wins — calm colour + live reach. Instantly legible to a prospect.
  • Sharp domain questions — signals we take their complexity seriously (the exact story they're buying).
  • Show, don't just tell, the simple↔advanced split — the differentiator vs Comarch's "powerful but complex."
  • Tie to positioning — every screen framed as "market-ready, not 20 years old."
